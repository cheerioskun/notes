# A History of Software

A 16-slide reading and discussion of Chapter 4 of *The Art of Doing Science and Engineering*.

Open `index.html` directly, or serve this directory with any static HTTP server. There is no build step, dependency install, analytics, or third-party runtime request. Fonts and illustrations are local.

Arrow keys and Space advance the presentation. O opens the slide overview, R toggles reading view, F toggles fullscreen, and B blanks the display. The question-and-answer slides remain separate. Text entrance animations never advance a slide. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Portrait phones open in reading view. Printing includes all 16 slides.

Artwork: AI-generated editorial illustrations, confined to the cover, Trusting Trust, and closing slide. The pocket terminal is the recurring character. Slide text is HTML, separate from the artwork.

Fonts: Libre Caslon Text and DM Sans, distributed under the SIL Open Font License; license files are included in `assets/`.
