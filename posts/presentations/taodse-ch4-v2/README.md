# A History of Software

A separate 16-slide presentation of Chapter 4 of Richard Hamming's *The Art of Doing Science and Engineering*. The earlier presentation remains at `../taodse-ch4/`.

Serve the notes repository with any static server. There is no build step. Type, tables, quotations, and presenter notes remain editable HTML. This edition shares the earlier deck's local font and artwork assets.

Arrow keys and Space advance one reveal at a time. Shift + arrows skip slides. O opens the overview, N shows presenter notes and full quotations, R switches to reading view, F enters fullscreen, and B blanks the screen. Home and End jump to the first and last slide. Touch screens support horizontal swipes. Portrait phones open in reading view. Reduced-motion settings disable animation.

Reading view and the landscape print layout show every reveal. The help panel includes Print / save PDF. The three fictional editorial illustrations are AI-generated and reused from the earlier edition. Fonts are Libre Caslon Text and DM Sans, with licenses in the original assets directory.
